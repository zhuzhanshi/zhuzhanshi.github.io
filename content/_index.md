    Zhanshi Zhu is a Postdoctoral Researcher at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), working with [Yanda Meng](https://yanda-meng.github.io/). His research lies at the intersection of continual learning and medical image analysis, with a current focus on ophthalmic AI. His broader research interests include medical image segmentation, foundation models, and robust learning under distribution shifts.

    Before joining KAUST, he received his B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees from [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://www.hit.edu.cn/), where he was affiliated with the [Perception Computing Lab](https://github.com/PerceptionComputingLab) and advised by [Kuangquan Wang](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/wangkuanquan), [Gongning Luo](https://luogongning.github.io/), and [Shuo Li](https://case.edu/engineering/about/faculty-and-staff-directory/shuo-li). He also worked on dynamic PET medical imaging at [Shanghai United Imaging Healthcare Technology Co., Ltd.](https://www.united-imaging.com/) under the supervision of [Yun Zhou](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=Bf2Bcg8AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate).
